1. Executive Scope
Why this section exists: Explains the prototype promise, boundaries and build rule so every reader starts with the same definition of what SIH success means.
e-Maap turns package photographs into source-grounded compliance evidence, preserves officer control over every finding and converts repeated verified results into coordinated government action. It is designed primarily for Legal Metrology inspectors and government officers; the citizen route supplies additional structured leads.
Build rule: a flagship claim is accepted only when it is enforced by backend logic, visible in the user journey, persisted as an auditable state and covered by an acceptance test. The differentiation therefore begins with implementation, not with the final presentation.

4. Flagship System Behaviour: Evidence to Verified Government Action
Why this section exists: Defines the end-to-end flagship behaviour as code-enforced workflow rather than presentation language.
This section is an implementation contract, not a pitch description. The flagship is complete only when the behaviours below are enforced in code and can be demonstrated from a real case record.
Technical flagship: Evidence-Completeness-Aware Multi-View AI
Why this section exists: Explains how the system gathers enough grounded evidence before it is allowed to reason about compliance.
Applicable versioned rules create an evidence plan before capture. The app gathers complementary views, checks quality and coverage, extracts candidate declarations, fuses repeated readings and exposes unresolved conflicts. Deterministic checks operate only on grounded facts; insufficient evidence produces a next-capture instruction or abstention, not a forced verdict.
Inspector output: Visual Compliance Map
Why this section exists: Defines the inspector-facing evidence index that connects declarations, sources, rules, uncertainty and officer action.
The map is a package-level visual index. Every declaration links to its source photograph, bounding box, fused value, evidence quality, applicable rule/version and officer action. This gives a fast visual overview without replacing the audit trail with a coloured score.
Technical flagship implementation contract
Why this section exists: Turns the flagship concept into non-negotiable invariants that code and tests must enforce.
[TABLE]
System invariant | Implementation requirement | Proof visible in the app
Plan before capture | Rule and package-profile configuration generates required evidence areas and useful views before the camera journey starts | The capture screen asks for the next evidence-bearing view, not a fixed front/back checklist
Coverage before absence | A declaration may enter Potentially absent only after the validated profile reports adequate coverage or the Inspector confirms coverage in Other Package mode | Missing coverage produces a targeted re-capture prompt; it cannot silently become a violation
Source before rule | A value is eligible for deterministic validation only when at least one photograph and bounding box are attached | Tapping any map value opens the exact supporting region
Preserve uncertainty | Conflicting readings, poor quality and obscured regions remain explicit states; fusion cannot silently overwrite them | Alternatives and their sources remain available for officer comparison
Human before final finding | Potential findings require an authenticated Accept, Correct or Reject transition with officer and reason | No final report finding exists before the officer action
Provenance by default | Capture automatically records case/Inspector IDs, sequence, GPS/accuracy, capture/sync times and SHA-256; local sync is idempotent | The Inspector sees simple Local, Uploading, Synced or Retry status while the audit record retains the detail
[/TABLE]
Government flagship: Verified Pattern-to-Action
Why this section exists: Explains how verified inspection findings become explainable systemic proposals without automating departmental authority.
After officer verification, related findings are correlated by product, batch, manufacturer, retailer, location and violated rule. Meaningful recurrence creates an explainable proposal. An authorised officer decides whether to form a systemic investigation, assign coordinated work and track follow-up.

Why the combination is differentiating
Why this section exists: Makes clear what is technically distinctive about the governed evidence-to-action chain beyond OCR or a dashboard.
Most prototypes stop at OCR, a compliance score or a polished dashboard. This prototype closes two practical gaps in one controlled chain: it prevents evidence gaps at the package level and intelligence gaps at the government level. The novelty is the operational architecture that connects evidence completeness, source-grounded rules, human verification and verified cross-case action - not the mere presence of a vision model or chatbot.
Practical impact: the Inspector spends less time deciding what to photograph, re-reading labels and retyping reports; the supervising officer sees recurring verified risk early and can coordinate one focused investigation instead of treating every case as isolated.

6. Unified Mobile App: Secured Inspector Pathway
Why this section exists: Defines the primary field workflow for capturing defensible evidence, synchronising it and completing officer verification.
The Inspector pathway is the centre of the prototype. It is designed to preserve defensible field evidence even when connectivity is temporarily unavailable, then complete server analysis, officer verification and reporting after synchronisation.
Core field journey
Why this section exists: Shows the inspector task sequence and the system assistance expected at each stage.
[TABLE]
Stage | Inspector action | System assistance
Open case | Review and download one assigned case | Load its package/declaration checklist for field use
Capture | Follow angled overview and close-up prompts | Assess basic blur, glare and coverage on-device; attach provenance automatically
Synchronise | Upload the locally preserved evidence when connected | Verify every item, prevent duplicates and start server processing
Review map | Inspect declaration states and conflicts | Fuse candidates and link each value to its source
Verify | Accept, correct, reject or add a finding | Preserve reason and evidence association
Report | Confirm completion | Generate the inspection report from the verified record
[/TABLE]
Package-adaptive capture plan
Why this section exists: Defines how capture guidance changes by package shape so evidence completeness is not reduced to a fixed photo checklist.
[TABLE]
Package form | Overview coverage | Targeted evidence
Rigid cuboid | Angled corner views for cartons, boxes and rectangular packs; top/bottom when applicable | Perpendicular close-up of each required declaration region
Curved rigid | Overlapping rotational views for bottles, cans, jars and cylinders; top/bottom when applicable | Close-up after the target area is centred to reduce curvature distortion
Flexible | Front/back angled views for pouches, sachets and packets plus seam/edge coverage | Flattened, glare-controlled close-up without hiding damage or obstruction
Other Package | Inspector records multiple overview views and manually confirms inspected areas | Targeted close-ups are processed, but automatic completeness is not claimed
[/TABLE]
The three validated families are configuration-driven capture profiles, not three individual products. A fixed front/back/left/right checklist is avoided because edges, curves, folds, damage and label continuation can hide declarations. Other Package mode keeps the Inspector operational while clearly requiring manual coverage confirmation.
Visual Compliance Map evidence states
Why this section exists: Defines the limited evidence-state vocabulary the inspector must see and resolve consistently.
[TABLE]
State | Meaning | Required response
Supported | Clear grounded evidence supports the result | Officer reviews and verifies
Conflicting | Different views or extractors disagree | Officer selects/corrects using the sources
Unresolved | A candidate exists but cannot be trusted | Capture targeted evidence or enter manually
Obscured / damaged | The required region cannot be read | Record condition and officer observation
Potentially absent | Coverage is adequate but no declaration was detected | Officer confirms absence; AI alone cannot do so
Not applicable | The selected rule does not require the field | Store the applicability reason
[/TABLE]
Evidence Provenance Seal
Why this section exists: Specifies the metadata and integrity record that makes each inspector photograph traceable from field capture to server use.
Every Inspector photograph receives provenance automatically. This strengthens traceability without adding form fields to the capture screen.
[TABLE]
Provenance element | Recorded value | Purpose
Identity | Case ID, Inspector ID and evidence sequence number | Associates the photograph with the authorised inspection
Place | GPS latitude/longitude and reported accuracy | Records where the field evidence was captured
Time | Device capture time and server synchronisation time | Preserves the field-to-server chronology
Integrity | SHA-256 fingerprint calculated at capture | Detects whether the registered image bytes later change
[/TABLE]
The seal establishes provenance and file integrity from capture; it is not represented as proof that GPS cannot be spoofed or that the photographed scene itself is genuine.
Bounded Offline Inspection Mode
Why this section exists: Defines exactly what remains usable without connectivity and what must wait for server synchronisation.
[TABLE]
Works without connectivity | Waits for synchronisation
Open one previously downloaded assignment | Full OCR and multimodal extraction
Use cached capture profile and declaration checklist | Cross-view fusion and deterministic rule validation
Capture photographs, observations and measurements | Visual Compliance Map assembly

Evidence-Grounded Compliance Copilot
Why this section exists: Defines the constrained officer assistant and the internal evidence sources it is allowed to use.
The Copilot is a desk-review and case-preparation assistant, not a generic chatbot. It answers only from permitted internal cases, officer-verified findings, versioned rules and images deliberately uploaded by an authorised user.
[TABLE]
Officer action | Grounded response
Summarise this case | Separates verified facts, citizen-reported claims, AI observations and missing evidence
Explain why it is prioritised | Shows severity, recurrence, verified history and evidence-readiness factors
Find related verified cases | Returns matching fields and direct links to the connected records
Analyse uploaded label images | Shows visible declarations, possible inconsistencies, conflicts and insufficient image coverage
What must be physically verified? | Converts evidence gaps and applicable rules into a focused capture checklist
Prepare inspection brief | Drafts product/case context, suspected issue, relevant rule, evidence needs and locations for officer approval
[/TABLE]
An absent declaration in an uploaded marketplace image is not treated as an established package violation: the image may simply omit that side. The Copilot must be able to return 'insufficient online-image evidence' and recommend physical verification.
Bounded Product Listing / Image Review
Why this section exists: Makes the problem-statement coverage of product images, listings and product information explicit while preventing an online screenshot from being treated as complete physical-package evidence.
This is a G2 case-review surface, not a new thirteenth capability and not a web-scraping subsystem. A Government officer deliberately uploads an image/screenshot and may supply product/listing text. The system analyses only what is actually visible or supplied, labels every source, and recommends physical verification whenever coverage is incomplete.
[TABLE]
Aspect | Prototype contract
Accepted input | Package/label photograph or e-commerce/listing screenshot; optional product name, listing text/details and source label; optional authorised case context.
Analysis | Run image quality, OCR, declaration extraction, source-region grounding, applicable bounded rules and FMT-01 format/inconsistency review on visible evidence. Supplied listing text is parsed separately and never masquerades as image evidence.
Output | Visible declarations, source regions, source-labelled supplied text, possible bounded format/inconsistency concerns, explicit evidence gaps and a physical-verification recommendation when required.
Absence guard | A declaration not visible in a listing image is NOT POTENTIALLY_ABSENT. Listing/image review never sets absence_eligible=true because unseen package sides may contain the declaration.
Persistence | When linked to an authorised case, store the uploaded image in the existing evidence model with LISTING_UPLOAD / GOVERNMENT_UPLOAD source metadata and retain source-linked observations. Otherwise the review may remain a transient officer analysis. No new product-master or marketplace database.
[/TABLE]

8. Explainable AI Technical Architecture
Why this section exists: Explains the AI/compliance pipeline stage by stage so every generated result remains inspectable and uncertainty is preserved.
The backend can be technically sophisticated while remaining understandable. Each stage produces an inspectable artifact, and uncertainty is carried forward rather than collapsed into a black-box score.
[TABLE]
Stage | Core method | Output shown or retained
1. Evidence planning | Versioned rules + package capture configuration | Required declarations, applicable checks and requested views
2. Capture quality | OpenCV geometry, blur, glare and overlap checks | Quality flags, coverage state and next-photo instruction
3. Region and text extraction | Computer vision + multilingual OCR | Candidate text, label region and bounding boxes
4. Entity parsing | Typed parsers with selective multimodal assistance | Manufacturer, net quantity, MRP, date and consumer-care candidates
5. Cross-view fusion | Normalisation and quality-weighted consensus | Selected value, alternatives, source ranking and conflicts
6. Rule validation | Deterministic versioned Python rules | Rule inputs, result state and explanation
7. Officer verification | Human-in-the-loop state transition | Accepted/corrected/rejected finding, reason and verifier
8. Pattern correlation | Explainable matching over verified findings | Related cases, matching factors and proposed investigation level
9. Officer Copilot | Tool-routed retrieval + constrained language generation | Source-linked explanation, evidence gap and editable inspection brief
[/TABLE]
AI explanation contract
Why this section exists: Defines the minimum grounding and explanation obligations for extraction, rule results and final officer-reviewed findings.
Every extracted value links to an image and bounding box.
Every legal result names the rule version and the evidence used.
Conflicting readings are exposed instead of silently averaged.
Insufficient coverage triggers re-capture or abstention.
Every final inspection finding records the officer's action and reason.
Why this is not ordinary OCR
Why this section exists: Clarifies the additional evidence, rule, verification and pattern reasoning that differentiates the system from text extraction alone.
OCR answers 'what text may be visible?' The prototype additionally asks: Was the required package area covered? Which reading is best supported across views? Which rule applies? Is the evidence complete enough to raise a finding? Who verified it? Does the same verified issue recur elsewhere? That governed chain is the technical contribution.
Case-Level Product Identity and Matching
Why this section exists: Defines the prototype-friendly identity model used for cross-case matching without building a central product-master system.
Each complaint or inspection stores its own identity snapshot: product name, brand, manufacturer/packer/importer, variant, net quantity, batch and package family. Officers confirm relevant fields. Pattern matching compares confirmed case snapshots dynamically and exposes the matching factors for officer confirmation, without requiring a separate product-master dataset.

19.1 AI-assisted coding guardrails
Why this section exists: States the non-negotiable rules an AI coder must preserve rather than replacing them with plausible alternative designs.
Do not create new user-facing capabilities, services or infrastructure that are not required by C1-C2, I1-I6 or G1-G4.
Do not invent new status names. Use the enums in Section 16 and map citizen-facing statuses separately.
Do not invent endpoint names or payload fields when an API contract exists; update the contract first if a genuine implementation gap is found.
Do not treat OCR/vision output as a verified legal finding. AI creates candidates and potential findings only.
Do not set POTENTIALLY_ABSENT unless the corresponding evidence-plan item is absence_eligible.
Do not discard conflicting extraction candidates; preserve source IDs and bounding boxes for officer comparison.
Do not mark an evidence item SYNCHRONISED until server receipt and hash validation succeed.
Do not create duplicate evidence records on retry; reuse the stable evidence UUID.
Do not count citizen leads, unverified AI observations or rejected findings in verified systemic-pattern counts.
Do not let Copilot output create a systemic case, approve a proposal or assign an inspector by itself.
Do not add a separate product-master, vector database, microservice split, WebSocket layer or full offline AI path for the SIH core unless the architecture is explicitly changed.
Do not infer physical font size from pixels unless a valid calibration source is attached; return UNMEASURABLE_FONT_SIZE instead.
Do not convert a blurred/glare-heavy photograph into a label-readability finding; image quality failure must first request re-capture.
Do not add Elasticsearch, embeddings or a second product database for repository search; use the indexed PostgreSQL read model in Section 16.5.
Do not implement a generic AI “misleading declaration detector.” Only FMT-01 reason-coded conditions encoded in this SRS may be raised automatically; unsupported semantic/legal interpretation must return REVIEW_REQUIRED_UNSUPPORTED_SEMANTIC.
Do not scrape marketplaces or add Amazon/Flipkart/e-commerce integrations for the Core prototype. W-GOV-08 accepts deliberate officer uploads and optional supplied text only.
Do not let a listing screenshot or other partial online image satisfy coverage-before-absence. Missing-from-view means evidence gap / physical verification required, not POTENTIALLY_ABSENT.

20. Final Build-Ready SRS Handoff Contract
Why this section exists: Explains how a teammate or AI coding model must interpret this single document and what it may or may not decide during implementation.
This section defines how a teammate or AI coding model must interpret this single document during implementation. The goal is to eliminate invention of product, legal, workflow, architecture, data, API, security and visual decisions while still allowing normal software engineering judgement where the prototype must be calibrated or where an internal code choice has no effect on the external contract.
Build-ready does not mean “zero implementation decisions whatsoever.” No useful software specification should freeze every helper function, threshold or library call. It means that every decision capable of changing what the prototype means, stores, exposes, permits or shows is either locked in this SRS or explicitly identified below as a tunable parameter. Anything else is ordinary implementation discretion inside the approved architecture.
20.1 Decision Ownership: Locked, Tunable and Implementation-Discretion
Why this section exists: Separates product/system decisions that are fixed from empirical values that require tuning and harmless local coding choices.
UI interpretation rule: SCREEN SCOPE / CONTENT / ACTIONS / STATE MEANING are locked contracts. The wireframe artwork itself is not locked pixel geometry. Visual craftsmanship is implementation discretion only inside the approved departmental design system; it may make the interface cleaner and more polished, but it may not invent a new workflow, hide required evidence, change a role permission or alter semantic status meaning.
[TABLE]
Decision class | Coder / AI behaviour | Examples in this SRS
LOCKED CONTRACT | Implement exactly as specified. A change requires updating this SRS before changing code. Do not silently “improve” or reinterpret it. | 12 capability scope; role separation; state names/transitions; evidence-before-rule and coverage-before-absence; officer verification; verified-only patterns; ERD relationships/enums; API paths/roles/guards; screen IDs, required screen content/navigation and wireframe information hierarchy; semantic visual meanings; offline boundary; T1-T17 and VUI-01..VUI-08 checks.; calibrated-only physical font-size rule; presentation abstention states; PostgreSQL-only repository/search contract.; FMT-01 bounded non-standard/inconsistency semantics; listing-review evidence-gap/no-unseen-absence guard; deliberate-upload-only e-commerce scope.
TUNABLE / EMPIRICAL | Keep the value in configuration, not as an unexplained magic constant. Choose an initial value only by running the representative fixtures/device flow, observe behaviour, and adjust until the defined acceptance behaviour is stable. Tuning may change without rewriting the SRS only when no locked semantic/invariant changes. | Blur/glare quality thresholds; OCR confidence bands; cross-view fusion similarity/tolerance; device-specific image limits; analysis timeout/retry timing; token lifetime; other runtime calibration values explicitly listed in 20.2.
IMPLEMENTATION DISCRETION | The coder may choose the local technique inside the approved stack when it cannot change the external contract. Prefer the simplest maintainable implementation; do not add infrastructure to solve a local code problem. | Private helper names; internal function decomposition; SQLAlchemy query form; small React component decomposition; unit-test file organisation; exact OpenCV implementation used to calculate a configured quality metric; pixel-level visual refinement within Sections 18.3/18.8 (spacing/alignment/responsive composition/icon treatment) so long as low-fidelity wireframes are not treated as final styling.
SPECIFICATION GAP | If a missing choice would alter user-visible behaviour, legal interpretation, a state transition, stored data, API semantics, permissions, system boundary or visual meaning, do not guess. Flag it as SPEC_DECISION_REQUIRED and resolve/update the SRS first. | A new status, new endpoint/payload field, new legal rule, new automatic government action, new persistent entity, different role permission, or a new visual meaning for green.
[/TABLE]
Specification precedence: the prototype framing boundaries and system invariants in Sections 2-4 and 10 remain authoritative. Sections 15-20 make those decisions implementation-ready and may clarify earlier high-level wording, but must not weaken human control, evidence provenance, uncertainty preservation, verified-only pattern logic or the bounded SIH scope. When two statements appear inconsistent, the coder must choose neither interpretation silently; surface the conflict as SPEC_DECISION_REQUIRED.
20.2 Tunable Prototype Configuration Registry
Why this section exists: Lists runtime thresholds intentionally left empirical so they can be calibrated on fixtures rather than guessed or hard-coded.
The values below are intentionally not treated as statutory or product truths. They are empirical prototype settings. A teammate or AI coder may calibrate them, but must externalise them in configuration and preserve the behaviour in the “Must preserve” column. The final demo configuration should be frozen once the fixture set and target devices are stable.
[TABLE]
Configuration key / family | How to choose or calibrate | Must preserve
capture.blur_threshold | Run the clean, difficult and deliberately blurred fixture images on the target mobile device/OpenCV pipeline. Pick the smallest threshold that reliably separates the deliberate blur case without rejecting normal usable evidence. | Poor blur must produce a specific re-capture instruction; it must never become a legal finding.
capture.glare_threshold | Calibrate on the selected reflective/glare fixtures and real package captures under demo lighting. | Glare may warn/re-capture or reduce evidence usability; it must not fabricate absence or a final finding.
capture.coverage_thresholds.<profile> | Calibrate per RIGID_CUBOID, CURVED_RIGID and FLEXIBLE profile using the seeded evidence-plan fixtures. OTHER_PACKAGE remains manual-confirmation only. | coverage_state and absence_eligible must still obey Section 15.1; POTENTIALLY_ABSENT cannot be reached before the gate passes.
ocr.accept_confidence / ocr.review_confidence | Measure PaddleOCR output on the seeded clean/difficult package images. Set bands so reliable candidates are usable and weak candidates are retained for re-capture/manual review. | Low confidence becomes UNRESOLVED/needs evidence; it is never silently accepted or discarded.
fusion.string_similarity / fusion.numeric_tolerance | Use deliberately repeated and conflicting views from the fixture set. Tune normalization/tolerance only to consolidate equivalent readings. | Two credible materially different readings outside the calibrated tolerance remain CONFLICTING with both source references.
upload.max_image_bytes / image target dimensions | Choose for target phone memory, network and S3/demo environment after testing the largest representative capture. Client and server limits must agree. | Original evidence remains traceable; resizing/compression must not destroy the registered evidence workflow or hide a hash mismatch.
analysis.poll_interval_ms | Use the existing contract of approximately 2-3 seconds while the analysis screen is active; minor adjustment for responsiveness/load is allowed. | No WebSocket/Redis layer is introduced; polling stops on SUCCEEDED/FAILED or when the screen leaves context.
analysis.timeout_seconds / max_retries | Choose from measured demo hardware/model latency. A timeout must be long enough for representative difficult fixtures but finite enough to recover in the demo. | Timeout/failure produces an explicit retryable state; the system never fabricates a completed analysis.
auth.jwt_ttl_minutes | Set per demo environment/security practicality and keep in environment configuration. | Expired/revoked credentials must not retain access; server-side RBAC remains authoritative.
priority.severity_rank.<rule_id> | Seed explicitly for the demo fixture set by the team/product owner. Do not infer a legal severity from model confidence or have the coder invent one in code. | Priority remains explainable and non-statutory; officer override with reason remains available.
presentation.measurement_tolerance_mm | Calibrate on seeded reference-marker/known-scale fixtures and target camera. It compensates measurement noise only; it must not replace or relax the legal minimum stored in the versioned rule record. | Physical font-size PASS/POTENTIAL concern is allowed only with valid calibration; legal threshold remains locked rule data.
presentation.readability_contrast_threshold / ocr_stability | Calibrate on quality-passing readable vs deliberately low-legibility fixture regions after blur/glare gating. | Poor capture still routes to re-capture; readability concern is raised only on otherwise usable source evidence.
presentation.placement_tolerance | Calibrate geometric tolerance on the selected seeded package/view fixtures to absorb normal capture variance around the configured placement zone. | Configured rule/zone meaning is not changed; if geometry is insufficient the result is INSUFFICIENT_PRESENTATION_EVIDENCE.
[/TABLE]
Locked prototype configuration is not part of this tuning permission. In particular, the pattern candidate trigger in Section 15.2 (minimum verified recurrence and retailer spread), the 3 validated package families + Other Package mode, D01-D06 declaration groups, LMPC-R01..R14 seeded checks, verified-only counting rule, one downloaded offline assignment boundary, and all human-approval gates remain locked unless this SRS is revised.
20.3 Configuration and “No Magic Constants” Rule
Why this section exists: Defines where tunable values live and how to change them without scattering unexplained literals through the codebase.
Empirical thresholds and operational timing values must live in a single prototype configuration source for the backend/demo environment. Do not scatter duplicated literals through routes, services and UI components.
Package-profile capture configuration and any on-device quality values needed offline are downloaded/cached with the assignment configuration so the Inspector app uses the same versioned profile rather than inventing its own values.
Versioned legal rules and package/evidence-plan fixture data belong in seed/config records that the backend can inspect and persist; do not encode legal text as unexplained conditionals inside UI code.
Mobile and Government web share semantic visual token names from Section 18.3/18.8. Platform-specific token files may differ technically, but the meaning and colours must not drift.
Secrets, credentials and deployment-specific keys live in environment variables or secret storage. They are never committed as prototype constants or embedded in this SRS.
When a tunable is changed during calibration, record the final demo value in the project configuration/seed file. The SRS needs revision only if the change alters a locked behaviour or interpretation.
20.4 Coding and Module Conventions
Why this section exists: Keeps generated and teammate-written code aligned with the approved architecture, state vocabulary and simplicity boundary.
[TABLE]
Area | Required convention
Backend modules | Keep the existing FastAPI modular-monolith boundaries: identity/roles; cases/assignments; capture/evidence; compliance; verification/reports; patterns/workflow; grounded Copilot. A module may have internal files/services, but do not split it into deployable microservices for the Core SIH prototype.
Data contracts | Pydantic/API schemas and PostgreSQL enums use the names defined in Sections 16-17. Frontends consume typed contracts; do not maintain competing status vocabularies.
Identifiers/time | Use UUIDs as contracted; offline evidence gets its stable UUID before upload. Server timestamps are ISO-8601 UTC while device capture time is also preserved.
State changes | State transitions occur through backend service/domain logic, not by arbitrary database updates from route handlers or frontend assumptions. Required audit events are written with the transition.
Frontend structure | Screens use stable IDs/routes from Section 18. Shared components implement the component specifications. Screen code uses the typed API client and explicit async/offline states rather than constructing ad-hoc URLs or response shapes.
Offline implementation | Use expo-sqlite + SQLCipher/app-private file storage only for the bounded assignment/evidence/sync subset. Full OCR/rule/pattern processing remains server-side after synchronisation.
Tests/fixtures | Use deterministic representative fixtures. Name/organise tests so T1-T17, role-negative checks and VUI-01..VUI-08 can be traced to code. A successful happy path alone is not Definition of Done.
Simplicity rule | When two implementations satisfy the same locked contract, choose the simpler one with fewer moving parts. Do not add Redis, WebSockets, event buses, vector stores, product-master services or extra infrastructure merely because a coding model suggests them.
[/TABLE]
20.5 AI-Assisted Coding Execution Protocol
Why this section exists: Defines the step-by-step behaviour expected when an AI coding model encounters locked contracts, tunables, discretion or a genuine specification gap.
When this document is provided to an AI coding model, the following execution protocol is part of the prompt/contract. It exists to prevent the model from filling a genuine specification gap with a plausible but competing design.
Start from the capability ID being implemented and read its row in Section 19 traceability before generating code.
Read the referenced rule/evidence behaviour, ERD/schema, API contract, screen/component specification and acceptance test. Implement those names and states; do not rename them for preference.
If an unknown value is listed in Section 20.2 as TUNABLE, create/use the configuration key, run the relevant fixtures or measured flow, and calibrate the value. Do not convert it into a hidden magic constant.
If the missing choice is not explicitly tunable and would change user-visible behaviour, legal meaning, workflow, data, API, permissions, system boundary or visual semantics, stop and report SPEC_DECISION_REQUIRED instead of guessing.
If the choice is purely internal and falls under IMPLEMENTATION DISCRETION, choose the simplest option inside the approved stack and continue without expanding scope.
For frontend work, treat the Section 18.2 wireframes as structure references, not a pixel-perfect target. Preserve the required screen content, actions, hierarchy and states, then implement a polished restrained departmental UI using the visual rules and tokens in Sections 18.3 and 18.8. Do not “beautify” by adding new workflows, decorative dashboards or semantic colour meanings.
Generate or update tests alongside the capability, tied to its T/VUI/role-negative acceptance IDs. Do not declare completion from a rendered screen alone.
Before finishing a capability, verify that backend guard + persisted artifact + visible UI behaviour + acceptance proof all exist, matching the build rule in Section 1.
20.6 Final Handoff Gate
Why this section exists: Provides the final checklist that must pass before the document can be treated as the authoritative implementation source for the demo build.
[TABLE]
Gate | Pass condition
Single source of truth | This Final Build-Ready SRS is the authoritative prototype specification. Separate notes/mockups may assist implementation but cannot silently override it.
No competing business design | No new capability, legal interpretation, state, workflow, data entity, endpoint, role permission, approval path or visual status meaning has been invented in code.
Tunables externalised | Every empirical/runtime threshold that affects prototype behaviour is visible in configuration and was calibrated against representative fixtures/device behaviour; no unexplained magic value controls a legal/evidence decision.
Traceability complete | Each C1-C2, I1-I6 and G1-G4 capability resolves to rule/behaviour + persisted data + API + screen/component state + acceptance proof. G2 additionally resolves listing/image review to COP-02/W-GOV-08/T17; bounded non-standard/inconsistency review resolves through FMT-01/T16.
Human-control invariants | AI output remains candidate/potential; officer verification and government approval boundaries are preserved; citizen/AI-only leads never inflate verified pattern counts.
Prototype boundary preserved | No microservice split, vector database, autonomous judiciary/legal action, full offline AI, central product master or unrelated enterprise infrastructure has been introduced.
Test proof | T1-T17, role-negative tests and VUI-01..VUI-08 pass on the deployable prototype with the frozen demo fixtures/configuration.
Visual implementation fidelity | The final UI is polished and coherent within Sections 18.3/18.8; low-fidelity wireframes are used as structural references rather than copied literally.
